Chemical Reviews

Welcome. This site collects my reviews of the chemical literature: critical, referenced write-ups that synthesize what the primary literature says about a given topic, rather than single-experiment reports.

What is a chemical review, here?

A review in this sense is not a rating or a recommendation — it's a survey of published research on a specific area of chemistry, aimed at answering questions like: what is currently understood, where do different studies agree or disagree, and what remains open. Each one is built by reading across the primary literature and pulling the findings together into a single, referenced account.

Have a look through the Reviews, or read more about why I write them on the About page.
